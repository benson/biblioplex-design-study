Working brief · September 9, 2026

The working archive

The richness of fantasy publishing. The pleasure of keeping physical objects. The directness of personal software.

The cards are the treasure

Artwork and information stay important. The surrounding material should make them feel collected and cared for.

Rich at the edges, clear at the center

Give framing, headings, and special surfaces room for expression. Keep repeated rows and forms comfortable to use.

Materials have a job

Paper holds records, a frame contains them, an inset field invites writing. Depth should describe the interface.

Dense can be beautiful

Use alignment, proportion, typography, and rules to make a dense collection legible.

Test the feeling through use

Compare sorting, narrow columns, real artwork, empty results, and an edit dialog. We’ll choose treatments here, then verify them in the production components.

Current direction

B’s material character; the exact supplied palettes; medieval column lettering; no repeated label beneath a full-word heading. Everything else is still open to refinement.
